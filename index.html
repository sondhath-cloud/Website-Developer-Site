About Sondra Hathaway

My career spans both public and private sectors, where I've focused for two decades on transforming organizational potential into reality.
For example I have:

  • Partnered with leaders through 30+ major reorganizations, cultural transformations, and optimization projects.
  • Created change playbooks used by more than a dozen organizations to scale learning and sustain change efforts.
  • Led organization-wide succession planning using 9-Box methodology during $9.45B company acquisition.
  • Deployed and analyzed employee engagement surveys across multiple organizations, translating results into executive-level recommendations.
  • Established and scaled Change Champion networks in 14 organizations, engaging 300+ Champions across private and public sector agencies.
  • Conducted change readiness assessments and impact analyses, reducing rollout risks by 40%.
  • Reduced help desk tickets by 55% in first 90 days post-launch by enlisting, enabling, and empowering Champions as first-line support.
  • Led implementation of multiple enterprise and learning management systems including Workday, Oracle, SAP, TargetSolutions, SumTotal, and Cornerstone.
